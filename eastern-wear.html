FIT & CARE

Find your perfect fit.

Our silhouettes honor classic South Asian draping while accommodating modern lifestyles. Every 3-piece suit, lawn ensemble, and embroidered shirt comes with generous inner seam allowances, allowing tailored adjustments whenever you wish. Hand-embroidered garments should be dry cleaned or delicately hand washed in cold water to preserve the luster of every thread.
